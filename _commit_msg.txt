fix: resize and redesign navbar logo for dark UI

Problem: full stacked logo (S icon above text) was illegible at navbar
height — the white pill showed a tiny unreadable lockup.

Solution:
- Add public/icon-mark.svg — the S mark extracted from the full logo
  with the dark lower curve changed to white so it reads on dark backgrounds
  (purple upper curve + white lower curve, transparent bg, viewBox cropped
  to the S mark region of the 1024x1024 source)
- Navbar now shows icon-mark.svg at h-8 (32px) alongside "SportHouse"
  in white HTML text — removes the white pill container entirely
- Result is a clean purple/white S icon + white wordmark that matches the
  dark glass navbar aesthetic

Design note: for future brand work, a horizontal lockup (S mark inline
with "SportHouse" wordmark on one line) is the standard asset for navbars
and horizontal contexts; the current stacked lockup is better suited to
square/icon contexts (app icons, favicons, stickers).
